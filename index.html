Simple Pinterest for Little Kids

What are you plans today? What will you make?

Big ideas start with a little imagination and having creativity! Pick an activity, use many colors, and make it your own!

Art

Invent a New Animal

Combine two animals into one new creature. Draw it with many colors and give it a funny name!

Crafts

Paper Flower Garden

Fold colorful paper into flowers. Arrange them on a page to create your own garden.

Building

Build a Small and Unique Town!

Use small cardboard boxes to make houses (cutting square cutouts in each box to make windows), shops, and a school. Add paper roads, coloring them grey.

Writing

The Secret Door

You find a tiny door behind your bookshelf or even behind a tree. Write a story about what is on the other side and what you will do.

Art

Nature Rubbings

Place a leaf under a sheet of paper. Gently rub a crayon (using it sideways) over it to reveal its pattern.

Crafts

Create a Paper Crown

Cut out paper strips from colored paper and decorate a paper strip with drawings and stickers. Ask an adult to help fit it into a crown using a stapler.
